# BD Toolkit Reference

## 1. Lead Qualification Framework

### BANT Quick Check (4 Questions)
| Criterion | Question | Green Flag | Red Flag |
|-----------|----------|------------|----------|
| Budget | "What budget range are you working with for this initiative?" | Allocated budget, fiscal year timing clear | "No budget yet", "Need to find budget" |
| Authority | "Who else would be involved in this decision?" | Decision maker in room or accessible | Multiple layers, unclear ownership |
| Need | "What's driving this initiative now?" | Specific pain, quantified impact | Exploratory, no urgency |
| Timeline | "When are you looking to have a solution in place?" | <6 months, tied to event/deadline | "Sometime next year", no driver |

### MEDDICC Simplified (For Complex Sales)
| Element | Key Question |
|---------|--------------|
| Metrics | "How will you measure success?" |
| Economic Buyer | "Who signs off on this budget?" |
| Decision Criteria | "What factors will determine your choice?" |
| Decision Process | "Walk me through your evaluation process." |
| Identify Pain | "What happens if you don't solve this?" |
| Champion | "Who internally is advocating for this?" |
| Competition | "What alternatives are you considering?" |

### 10 Qualification Questions (Discovery Call)
1. What triggered your interest in [solution area] right now?
2. What's the business impact of the current situation?
3. What have you tried before? What worked/didn't work?
4. Who else is affected by this problem?
5. What does success look like in 6-12 months?
6. What's your timeline for making a decision?
7. What's your budget range for this initiative?
8. Who else needs to be involved in this decision?
9. What would make you choose us over alternatives?
10. What's the next step that makes sense?

## 2. Outreach Templates

At [Your Company], we help [target segment] achieve [specific outcome] through [brief differentiator]. For example, [reference customer or data point] achieved [quantified result].

Would a 15-minute call next week make sense to explore if there's a fit?

Best regards,
[Your name]

## 3. Meeting Agendas

### 30-Minute Discovery Call

| Time | Topic | Owner |
|------|-------|-------|
| 0-3 min | Intro, confirm agenda, time check | You |
| 3-12 min | Understand their situation: current state, pain, triggers | Them |
| 12-20 min | Share relevant capabilities, reference stories | You |
| 20-25 min | Qualify: budget, timeline, decision process | Both |
| 25-30 min | Agree next steps, schedule follow-up | You |

**Pre-meeting ask:** "To make our time productive, could you share [current metrics/process doc/org chart]?"

---

### 45-Minute Solution Discussion

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Recap previous conversation, confirm goals for today |
| 5-15 min | Deep dive on 1-2 priority use cases |
| 15-30 min | Demo/walkthrough relevant to their use case |
| 30-40 min | Technical/commercial questions |
| 40-45 min | Define POC scope or next steps |

---

### 60-Minute Workshop / Technical Deep Dive

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0-5 min | Intros, agenda, desired outcomes |
| 5-20 min | Current state mapping (their process, pain points, metrics) |
| 20-40 min | Solution walkthrough with Q&A |
| 40-50 min | Integration, deployment, support discussion |
| 50-60 min | POC proposal, timeline, action items |

## 5. Pricing & Terms Framework

### Price Structure Options
| Model | Best For | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Per unit | Hardware, consumables | $X per kWh, per module |
| Subscription | Software, services | $X/month per user/site |
| Revenue share | Performance-based | X% of savings/revenue |
| Milestone-based | Projects | 30-40-30 payment schedule |
| Hybrid | Complex deals | Base + variable |

### Key Commercial Terms Checklist
- [ ] Price and payment schedule
- [ ] Delivery timeline and Incoterms
- [ ] Warranty period and scope
- [ ] Performance guarantees (if any)
- [ ] Liability caps
- [ ] IP ownership
- [ ] Confidentiality

## 6. Objection Handling

### Price Objections
| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Too expensive" | "Compared to what? Let's look at total cost of ownership and ROI." |
| "Competitor is cheaper" | "What's included in their price? Let me show you the value difference." |
| "No budget" | "When does your budget cycle reset? Can we structure payments differently?" |

### Risk Objections
| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Too risky/new" | "Here's how [reference customer] mitigated that. We can start with a limited POC." |
| "Need more references" | "I can connect you with [customer] who had similar concerns." |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | "Our POC has clear exit criteria. Here's our performance guarantee." |

### Timing Objections
| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Not now" | "What would need to change? What's the cost of waiting?" |
| "Need to think about it" | "Of course. What specific concerns should I address to help your decision?" |
| "Internal priorities" | "Who else should we involve to align this with priorities?" |

### Competition Objections
| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Already have a vendor" | "How's that working? Most clients use us for [specific gap]." |
| "Considering alternatives" | "What criteria matter most? Let me show how we compare on those." |

### Internal Politics Objections
| Objection | Response Framework |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Need boss approval" | "Happy to join that conversation. What does [boss] care most about?" |
| "Other departments involved" | "Who should we include? I can tailor the pitch for each stakeholder." |
