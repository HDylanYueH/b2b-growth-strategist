# Company Analysis Framework

## Purpose

This framework guides analysis of target companies **from "our company's" perspective**
(as defined in `config/company-profile.md`) to identify:
1. Product/technology alignment and gaps
2. Competitive overlap vs. partnership opportunities
3. Target-market engagement and entry points
4. Outreach strategy and messaging

## 5. Competitive vs. Partnership Assessment

### Overlap Matrix

For each item in `config/company-profile.md` Section 3 (Product/Service Portfolio),
classify the relationship:

| Our Product/Service (from profile §3) | Target Company Position | Relationship |
|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------|
| [Product/Service A] | ☐ Competes / ☐ Buys / ☐ Neither | |
| [Product/Service B] | ☐ Competes / ☐ Buys / ☐ Neither | |
| ... | | |

### Relationship Classification

**Competitor** (Overlap >50%):
- Direct product/market competition
- Strategy: Monitor, differentiate, or avoid

**Potential Customer** (Buys what we offer):
- Needs products/services we provide
- Strategy: Supplier positioning, technical/commercial qualification

**Potential Partner** (Complementary):
- Capability/market complementarity
- Strategy: Joint development, co-marketing, licensing

**Strategic Target** (Multiple dimensions):
- Could be customer AND partner
- Strategy: Multi-track engagement

## 7. Cold Outreach Templates

> 占位符说明 / Placeholder notes:
> - `[Our Company]` → fill from `config/company-profile.md` Section 1
> - `[our offering]` → fill from Section 3
> - `[differentiator]` → fill from Section 5

### Template A: Technical Collaboration (English)

**Subject:** [Our offering] — Potential Collaboration with [Company]

Dear [Name],

I noticed [Company]'s work in [specific area relevant to their business]. Given your
focus on [specific initiative], I thought it worth reaching out.

At [Our Company], we specialize in [our offering — from profile §3]. [Key
differentiator — from profile §5, e.g., "We're among the first to commercialize X
at industrial scale, with proven Y performance."]

Companies in [their segment] typically work with us for:
- [Value point 1 — from profile §6]
- [Value point 2]
- [Value point 3]

Would a brief call make sense to explore if there's potential alignment?

Best regards,
[Your name]

Best regards,
