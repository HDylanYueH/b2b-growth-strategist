# Market Analysis Frameworks

## 1. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)

### Definitions
| Term | Definition | Calculation |
|------|------------|-------------|
| TAM | Total Addressable Market - entire market demand | # of potential customers × average deal size |
| SAM | Serviceable Addressable Market - segment you can serve | TAM × % you can technically serve |
| SOM | Serviceable Obtainable Market - realistic capture | SAM × expected market share (typically 1-5% for new entrants) |

### Top-Down Approach
1. Start with industry reports (BNEF, Wood Mackenzie, IEA, etc.)
2. Filter by geography, segment, application
3. Apply your addressable % based on product fit

### Bottom-Up Approach
1. Identify target customer segments
2. Count potential customers in each segment
3. Estimate average deal size per segment
4. Multiply and sum

### Example (New Energy Storage)
```
TAM: Global C&I battery storage market = $15B (2025)
SAM: Europe + North America, 100kWh-10MWh systems = $4B
SOM: Year 1 realistic capture at 0.5% = $20M
```

## 4. Unit Economics

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Formula | Healthy Range |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Gross Margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | >40% for hardware, >70% for software |
| CAC | Total S&M cost / New customers | Varies by segment |
| LTV | (Avg revenue × Gross margin × Lifetime) | LTV:CAC > 3:1 |
| Payback Period | CAC / (Monthly revenue × Gross margin) | <18 months |

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy Options

### Direct vs. Indirect
| Approach | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|----------|------|------|----------|
| Direct sales | Control, margins, customer relationship | Expensive, slow to scale | High-value, complex sales |
| Channel/Reseller | Scale, local presence | Lower margin, less control | Geographic expansion |
| OEM/White-label | Volume, embedded | Very low margin, no brand | Commoditized components |
| Marketplace | Discovery, trust transfer | Fees, competition | Standard products |

### Entry Strategy Options
| Strategy | Description | Risk | Speed |
|----------|-------------|------|-------|
| Lighthouse customer | Win 1 marquee customer first | Medium | Slow |
| Geographic focus | Dominate one market, then expand | Low | Medium |
| Vertical focus | Own one industry segment | Low | Medium |
| Partner-led | Enter through established partner | Low | Fast |
| Price disruption | Undercut to gain share | High | Fast |

## 8. Data Sources for Market Research

### Industry Reports
- BloombergNEF (BNEF) - Energy transition
- Wood Mackenzie - Energy, metals
- IEA - Global energy outlook
- IRENA - Renewables
- GTM Research - Solar, storage
- Navigant / Guidehouse - Grid, utilities

### Company Research
- Annual reports, 10-K filings
- LinkedIn (org structure, hiring)
- Crunchbase (funding, investors)
- News, press releases
- Industry conference presentations

### Market Data
- Government statistics (EIA, Eurostat)
- Trade associations
- Patent filings
- Import/export data

### Validation Methods
| Method | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| Expert interviews | Qualitative insights, trends |
| Customer surveys | Demand validation, pricing |
| Competitor analysis | Positioning, gaps |
| Pilot/POC | Technical and commercial validation |
